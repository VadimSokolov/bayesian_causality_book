This appendix holds the proofs of results stated in the chapters. It covers noncollapsibility of the odds ratio, exchangeability in the finite and infinite cases, ignorability and the Bayesian posterior, a Bernstein von Mises theorem for causal functionals, tail robustness of the horseshoe, regularization induced confounding, the limit of Li's estimator, partially identified posteriors, the Makarov and Tian and Pearl bounds as linear programs, noise outsourcing and the weights of two way fixed effects. It ends with the proofs for the chapters on groups and urns, on DML and on its critique. Where a result is classical, the appendix gives a short proof for completeness and names the source.

## From the literature

The chapter presents or builds on the Bernstein von Mises theorem (van der Vaart 1998), the tail robustness of the horseshoe (Carvalho, Polson and Scott 2010; Polson and Sokolov 2026b), the Makarov bounds (Makarov 1981), the Tian and Pearl bounds (Tian and Pearl 2000).
