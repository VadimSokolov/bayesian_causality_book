This appendix gives worked solutions to the exercises that close most chapters, one paragraph for each exercise, in chapter order. The solutions derive identities, reproduce small numerical examples by hand, check simulation designs, and in some cases report short computations whose scripts are in the repository. They are meant for a reader teaching from the book or studying alone. Every exercise has a solution.

## From the literature

The exercises build on the results of the chapters they follow, which carry their own credits.
