This appendix is the notation reference for the book. It lists the symbols for potential outcomes, treatment and covariates, the treatment effect tau and the horseshoe global scale, the nuisance functions and fitting errors of DML, the posterior and its draws, and the groups and urns of Chapter 4, and it says where a symbol is reused with a different meaning in a different chapter. 

## From the literature

The notation follows common usage in the potential outcomes literature (Rubin 1974) and in the horseshoe literature (Carvalho, Polson and Scott 2010).
