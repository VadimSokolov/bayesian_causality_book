Two health applications close the applied chapters. The first estimates the effect of quitting smoking on weight gain over eleven years in the NHEFS cohort. Three Bayesian models agree with each other and with the published analysis, and a causal forest shows little heterogeneity by smoking intensity or age. The second analyses Thornton's randomized trial of cash incentives for learning HIV results in Malawi, with a logistic model, marginal and conditional odds ratios, risk differences and a dose response in the incentive. The Thornton analysis shows the accounting view at work: the same fitted model gives a conditional odds ratio and a different marginal one, and both are reported with their posterior.

## From the literature

The chapter presents or builds on the NHEFS analysis (Hernan and Robins 2020), the Thornton experiment (Thornton 2008), noncollapsibility (Greenland, Robins and Pearl 1999).
